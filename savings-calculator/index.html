Where do the default numbers come from?

Published industry benchmarks: 15–30% marketplace commissions (30–40% all-in), 2.2–3.5%+ average small-business processing costs, and 15–25% achievable utility reductions from demand management. Replace them with the numbers from your own statements for a real estimate.

Is this estimate guaranteed?

No — it's a model. Your actual savings depend on your statements, market, and contracts. That's exactly what the free audit verifies before you commit to anything.

Does this calculator store my data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or transmitted.
